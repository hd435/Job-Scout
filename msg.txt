Record how the hosted bot is actually kept alive

Measured on the pilot deployment rather than assumed:

- restart-on-failure alone does not resurrect the bot (killed by hand, task went
  Ready, nothing restarted after 90 seconds) and a Repetition attached to a
  logon trigger produced no NextRunTime. A separate time-based trigger repeating
  every two minutes with IgnoreNew does: the bot came back 59 seconds after
  being killed and a live one is never duplicated.
- scans block polling: eleven sources take 23 seconds, while Wise's first pass
  is partial (400 of 428 detail fetches) and takes minutes, then completes on
  its next pass by skipping unchanged pages. Documented so a slow start is not
  mistaken for a hang, and so nobody "fixes" the partial result.
